# Blink the Pico's onboard LED at 2 Hz

Target board: a Raspberry Pi Pico running MicroPython (one is leased to you by the farm; ask for a `pico` device).
Write `main.py` so the onboard LED (GP25) blinks at **2 Hz** (two full on/off cycles per second, 50% duty).
Print `READY` once the loop has started.

A camera watches the LED, so your `print` output proves nothing: verify with the farm before claiming you are done.
